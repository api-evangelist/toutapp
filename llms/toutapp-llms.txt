# ToutApp

> ToutApp was a sales-engagement platform (email templates, email tracking, campaigns, and sales analytics) founded in 2011 by Tawheed Kader and backed by Andreessen Horowitz. Marketo acquired ToutApp in May 2017 and folded it into Marketo Sales Connect, which became part of Adobe when Adobe acquired Marketo in 2018. ToutApp is defunct as a standalone company: toutapp.com now redirects to Adobe's Marketo Sales Connect page, and no independent ToutApp API or developer surface remains publicly available.
